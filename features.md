# Project Features

## SCRUM-203 - Patient Management System - Patient Registration, Records Management, and Appointment Scheduling

### Feature Summary
A centralized dashboard for healthcare staff to register new patients, view and update medical records, and schedule or modify patient appointments with healthcare providers.

### Key Features
- Patient Registration and Intake Portal
- Patient Medical Records Management
- Interactive Appointment Scheduling & Calendar
- Search, Filter, and Patient History Tracking
