# Project Features

## SCRUM-259 - Farm Management System Core Operations & Tracking

### Feature Summary
A full-stack Farm Management System that allows farm managers to track crop cycles, livestock health, equipment maintenance, and input inventory, optimizing farm productivity and operational efficiency.

### Key Features
- Field & Crop Cycle Management (planting, harvesting, soil health, field mapping)
- Livestock Health & Event Tracking (vaccinations, breeding, feeding logs, medical records)
- Equipment & Asset Maintenance (preventative maintenance scheduling, repair logs, status tracking)
- Input Inventory Management (fertilizer, seeds, pesticides stock levels, reorder alerts)
- Full-stack Dashboard & Analytics (role-based access, operational alerts, audit logs)
