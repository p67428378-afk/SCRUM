# Project Features

## SCRUM-251 - Tea Management System - Inventory, Order Processing, and Quality Tracking

### Feature Summary
Enables tea house staff and managers to track tea inventory, process customized customer orders, and access brewing recipes to ensure quality standards.

### Key Features
- Inventory & Stock Management with minimum reorder threshold alerts
- Order Processing & POS interface with custom sweetness, temperature, and add-on options
- Brewing Quality & Recipe Tracking with steeping parameters and feedback logging
